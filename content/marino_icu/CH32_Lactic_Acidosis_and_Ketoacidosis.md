## Chapter 32: Lactic Acidosis and Ketoacidosis

> Fundamental progress has to do with the reinterpretation of basic ideas.
> — Alfred North Whitehead（艾弗雷德·諾斯·懷特黑德）

This chapter focuses on the two most prominent acid-base disorders encountered in the ICU: lactic acidosis（乳酸中毒）and ketoacidosis（酮症酸中毒）. The problem with these disorders has little to do with the acid-base disturbances they promote. Also covered in this chapter is alkali therapy（鹼性療法）with bicarbonate（碳酸氫鹽），and the reasons to avoid it.

---

## Lactic Acidosis（乳酸中毒）

### Biochemistry of Lactate（乳酸的生物化學）

Lactate（乳酸）is a three-carbon anion formed by the chemical reduction of pyruvate（丙酮酸鹼基）in the cytoplasm（細胞質），in a reaction catalyzed by the lactate dehydrogenase (LDH)（乳酸脫氫酶）enzyme. About 1,500 mmol of lactate are produced daily under aerobic conditions（有氧條件）(1,2). The principal sites of production are:

- **Skeletal muscle（骨骼肌）:** 25%
- **Skin（皮膚）:** 25%
- **Red blood cells（紅血球）:** 20%
- **Brain（腦）:** 20%
- **Intestine（腸道）:** 10%

Activated neutrophils（活化的中性粒細胞）are an additional source of lactate in inflammatory conditions（發炎狀態）(3). The concentration of lactate in plasma（血漿乳酸濃度）is usually ≤ 2 mmol/L, with a lactate:pyruvate ratio（乳酸:丙酮酸比值）of 10:1 (1,2). Lactate is cleared from plasma by the liver（肝臟）(60%), kidneys（腎臟）(30%), and heart（心臟）(10%).

### The Evolving Lactate Paradigm（不斷演進的乳酸理論）

The traditional teaching has been that anaerobic conditions（缺氧狀態）are the principal source of increased lactate production. However, the description of lactate metabolism in Chapter 9 introduces the following notions:

1. **Lactate production is NOT driven by anaerobic metabolism**（乳酸生成並非由無氧代謝所驅動）(see Table 9.5).
2. **Lactate serves as an oxidative fuel（乳酸作為氧化燃料）** in conditions of metabolic stress（代謝壓力狀態），with a caloric yield（熱量產出）equivalent to glucose（葡萄糖）(see Table 9.6).

During conditions of metabolic stress when glucose availability is limited, lactate can provide:
- **60%** of the energy needs of the myocardium（心肌）(4)
- **30%** of the energy needs of the brain（腦）(5)

During exercise（運動期間），about three-quarters of the lactate produced is used as an oxidative fuel（氧化燃料），and in exercising muscle there is an intercellular lactate shuttle（細胞間乳酸穿梭）that transfers lactate from white "glycolytic" muscle fibers（白色「糖酵解」肌纖維）to red "oxidative" muscle fibers（紅色「氧化」肌纖維）(6).

### Lactate is Not an Acid（乳酸不是酸）

The traditional teaching has been that lactic acid（乳酸）is the end-product of glucose metabolism in the cytoplasm (glycolysis)（細胞質中的葡萄糖代謝終產物（糖酵解）），but there are a number of problems with this scenario, as indicated by the numbers in Figure 32.1 (7,8).

1. **The end-product of glycolysis is the lactate anion（乳酸陰離子），which is not an acid.**
2. **The chemical reaction that produces lactate actually consumes hydrogen ions（氫離子）**，which should have the opposite effect to the production of an acid.
3. **The hydrogen ions associated with lactate production** are attributed to the hydrolysis of the 2 ATP molecules formed during glycolysis (8), but how this influences extracellular pH（細胞外pH值）is unclear.

Each of these points is contrary to the traditional explanation for lactic acidosis. The fact that hyperlactatemia（高乳酸血症）can exist without an associated acidosis（酸中毒）(9) is further evidence of the incomplete understanding of the relationship between lactate production and acidosis.

### Prognostic Value（預後價值）

Regardless of its origins or function, plasma lactate levels（血漿乳酸濃度）have prognostic significance（預後意義）in critically ill patients（危重症患者）:

1. **The initial lactate level（初始乳酸濃度）** has a direct relationship with mortality rates（死亡率），as shown in the left-hand graph in Figure 9.4 (10,11).
2. **The time required for lactate levels to return to normal（乳酸濃度恢復正常的所需時間）** (lactate clearance（乳酸清除））is a better predictor of survival than the initial lactate level (10,11).
