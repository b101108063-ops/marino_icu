The intravenous route is used for nutrition support when the enteral route is not feasible, or is

### Substrate Solutions

Dextrose Solutions
Standard nutrition support regimens use carbohydrates to supply approximately 70% of the daily
(non蛋白質) calorie requirements. The carbohydrate source for total 腸胃外營養 (全靜脈營養（TPN）) is
葡萄糖（dextrose） (葡萄糖), which is available in the solutions shown in Table 50.1 Because the energy
yield from 葡萄糖（dextrose） is relatively low (see Table 48.1), the 葡萄糖（dextrose） solutions must be
concentrated to provide enough 熱量 to satisfy daily requirements. The standard solution is
50% 葡萄糖（dextrose） (D50), which provides 1,700 kcal per liter. Concentrated 葡萄糖（dextrose） solutions have a
very high 滲透壓 which mandates infusion through large, 中央靜脈s.

Amino Acid Solutions
Protein is provided as 胺基酸 solutions that contain mixtures of essential and nonessential
胺基酸s. (Note: Humans can synthesize 11 of the 20 胺基酸s that are used to build
蛋白質s. The remaining 9 胺基酸s, which are called essential 胺基酸s, must be provided
in the diet. These 胺基酸s include histidine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine,
threonine, tryptophan, and valine.)

Standard 胺基酸 solutions (e.g., Travasol®) are balanced mixtures of 50% essential
胺基酸s and 50% nonessential 胺基酸s. For full nutrition support, a 10% 胺基酸
solution will provides 100 grams of 蛋白質 per liter, or 16.5 grams of 氮 per liter. Amino
acid solutions can be mixed together with the 葡萄糖（dextrose） solutions and 脂肪 emulsions.

Branched-Chain Amino Acids
Amino acid solutions enriched with branched-chain 胺基酸s (isoleucine, leucine, and valine),
such as Aminosyn II® are designed for the following situations:

1. In progressive liver failure, aromatic 胺基酸s (i.e., phenylalanine, tryptophan, tyrosine)
have been implicated in the pathogenesis of 肝性腦病變. Branched-chain amino
acids competitively block the transport of aromatic 胺基酸s across the blood-brain barrier
(5), and can thereby reduce the risk of 肝性腦病變 (6).

2. Branched-chain 胺基酸s are metabolized in skeletal muscle, and parenteral formulas
enriched with branched-chain 胺基酸s are more likely to achieve a positive 氮
balance during periods of metabolic stress like trauma or 敗血症 (7).

However, despite these theoretical benefits, nutrition formulas enriched with branched-chain
胺基酸s have not improved outcomes in any of the conditions for which they are designed
(6,7).

Lipid Emulsions
Lipids are provided as emulsions composed of submicron droplets of cholesterol, phospho脂肪s,
and 三酸甘油酯. The 三酸甘油酯 are derived primarily from 大豆油, and the features of a
popular 大豆油 emulsion are shown in Table 50.2 (8,9). As indicated, these emulsions are
rich in essential 脂肪酸 (linoleic and linolenic acid), which must be provided in the diet, and
which can prevent essential fatty acid deficiency when they provide 3–4% of the daily caloric
requirement (10).

Lipids have the highest energy content (9 kcal/g) of the nutritional fuels, and 脂肪 emulsions
are used to provide about 30% of the daily energy needs. The soybean emulsions are available in
10% and 20% strengths (the percentage refers to grams of triglyceride per 100 mL of solution):
the 10% emulsions provide 1 kcal/mL, and the 20% emulsions provide 2 kcal/mL (8). Unlike the
hypertonic 葡萄糖（dextrose） solutions, 脂肪 emulsions are roughly isotonic to plasma, and can be infused
through 周邊靜脈s. This allows 脂肪s to be used as a major source of 熱量 in 蛋白質-
sparing nutritional regimens, which are delivered via 周邊靜脈s (as described later in the
chapter). However, for full 腸胃外營養 support, 脂肪 emulsions are added to the 葡萄糖
and 蛋白質 solutions to create a 3-in-1 nutrient admixture.

Newer Lipid Emulsions
The polyunsaturated 脂肪酸 in 大豆油 are needed to prevent essential fatty acid
deficiency, but they have also been implicated in the adverse effects of nutritional 脂肪s, which
include hypertriglyceridemia, inflammatory injury, and 脂肪 overload syndrome (which are
described later in the chapter). In an attempt to reduce the risk of these complications, 脂肪
emulsions have been introduced that incorporate less damaging 脂肪s. These newer products are
listed below (9).

1. Clino脂肪® is 80% 橄欖油 and 20% 大豆油. Olive oil contains medium-chain
三酸甘油酯, which are more readily metabolized, and less likely to promote the 脂肪 overload
syndrome (11).

2. Smof脂肪® is a mixture of 30% 大豆油, 30% medium-chain 三酸甘油酯, 20% 橄欖油,
and 15% 魚油. The 魚油 is rich in omega-3 脂肪酸, which are less likely to promote
inflammatory injury.

3. Omegaven® is pure 魚油, which has the benefits of omega-3 脂肪酸, but will not prevent

essential fatty acid deficiency unless combined with 脂肪s from 大豆油.

The experience with the above products is limited at the present time.

### Additives

Commercially available mixtures of 電解質, 維生素, and 微量元素 are added directly
to the 3-in-1 nutrient admixture.

Electrolytes
There are numerous electrolyte mixtures that are commercially available. Most have a volume of
20 mL, and contain sodium, chloride, potassium, and magnesium. You must check the mixture
used at your hospital to determine if additional 電解質 must be added. Most hospitals have
special order forms where the desired electrolyte changes can be specified.

Vitamins
Aqueous multivitamin preparations will provide the normal daily requirements for most 維生素
(see Table 48.3) (16). The daily vitamin requirements in ICU patients are likely to be higher than
normal, which explains why vitamin deficiencies are commonplace in ICU patients (see the
section on “Vitamin Requirements” in Chapter 48).

### Creating A Tpn Regimen

The following is a stepwise approach to creating a standard 全靜脈營養（TPN） regimen for an individual
patient. The patient in this example will be a 70 kg adult who is not malnourished and has no
volume restrictions.

Step 1
The initial task is to determine the daily requirement for 熱量 and 蛋白質. This is described in
Chapter 48, and is briefly reviewed here. The daily energy requirement, or resting energy
expenditure (休息能量消耗（REE）), can be measured by 間接測熱法, or it can be estimated using a
simple formula: 休息能量消耗（REE） = 25 × weight (in kg). The daily 蛋白質 requirements can be estimated as
1.2–2.0 grams per kg body weight. You can use actual body weight in these estimates as long as
actual body weight is not greater than 125% of the ideal weight (see Appendix 2 for ideal body
weights). If actual body weight exceeds 125% of ideal body weight, you can use the adjusted
body weight (which is derived in Equation 48.3).

For the 70 kg patient, we’ll use a daily 蛋白質 requirement of 1.4 g/kg. Therefore, the daily

requirement for 熱量 and 蛋白質 will be:

Note: If propofol is being infused, you should determine the 熱量 provided by propofol
and subtract those 熱量 from the daily caloric requirement. Propofol is infused in a 10% 脂肪
emulsion, which has a caloric density equivalent to 10% Intra脂肪 (1 kcal/mL). Therefore, the
hourly infusion rate of propofol (mL/hr) is equivalent to the hourly yield of 熱量 (kcal/hr).

Step 2
The next step is to determine the volume of the 胺基酸 solution needed to meet the daily
蛋白質 requirement. A 10% 胺基酸 solution has 100 grams of 蛋白質 per liter, so one liter of
the 胺基酸 solution will satisfy the estimated daily 蛋白質 requirement (i.e., 98 grams/day).

Step 3
The next step is to determine how to deliver the daily caloric requirement, which should be
provided as non蛋白質 熱量 in the 葡萄糖（dextrose） solution and 脂肪 emulsion. Start with the 脂肪
emulsion. The standard 脂肪 emulsion in many hospital pharmacies is a 20% emulsion, which
provides 2 kcal/mL. Therefore, 250 mL of a 20% 脂肪 emulsion will provide 500 kcal. That
leaves 1,750 – 500 = 1,250 kcal that must be provided by 葡萄糖（dextrose）. One liter of 50% 葡萄糖（dextrose）
provides 1,700 kcal, or 1.7 kcal/mL, so the volume of D50 needed to provide 1,250 kcal is
1,250/1.7 = 735 mL.

Therefore, the 腸胃外營養 formulation for this example would be an admixture of the

following:

1. 1 liter of 10% 胺基酸 solution
2. 250 mL of a 20% 脂肪 emulsion
3. 735 mL of a 50% 葡萄糖（dextrose） solution (D50)
4. 30 mL for 維生素, 電解質, and 微量元素

Step 4
The final step is determining the infusion rate of the nutrient admixture. The total volume of the
admixture is 2,015 mL, which is infused over 24 hours, so the hourly infusion rate is 2,015/24 =
84 mL/hr. However, in patients who are at risk for the 重新進食症候群 (see later), the
American Society of Parenteral and Enteral Nutrition recommends starting at 40–50% of the

nutritional goal, and advancing to full nutrition support as allowed by the serum levels of
phosphate, potassium, and magnesium (usually 4–7 days) (12). For this example, starting at 50%
of the nutritional goal corresponds to an infusion rate of 42 mL/hr.

### Complications

The following complications do not include those associated with central venous catheters,
which are required for full 腸胃外營養. Catheter-related complications are described in
Chapters 2 and 3.

#### Carbohydrate Complications

Hyperglycemia
Hyperglycemia is common during total 腸胃外營養 (全靜脈營養（TPN）): e.g., in one report, blood
葡萄糖 levels above 200 mg/dL were recorded in 45% of the patients receiving 全靜脈營養（TPN） (13). Tight
glycemic control is not recommended in critically ill patients because of the risk of
低血糖, which has more serious consequences than 高血糖 (14). The current
recommendation for hospitalized patients is a target range of 140–180 mg/dL for blood 葡萄糖
(15).

INSULIN: Insulin is often necessary for glycemic control during 全靜脈營養（TPN）, and Table 50.4 shows the
variety of biosynthetic 胰島素s that are available (16). A continuous infusion of regular 胰島素 is
preferred for critically ill patients (17), and this can often be accomplished by adding regular
胰島素 to the feeding admixture, and using subcutaneous 胰島素 (lispro) as needed for glycemic
control. When patients are clinically stable, half of the daily 胰島素 requirement can be
converted to long-acting glargine 胰島素, which is given once daily.

Inflammatory Injury

The 大豆油 used in standard 脂肪 emulsions is rich in omega-6 and omega-9
polyunsaturated 脂肪酸 that serve as precursors to the formation of eicosanoids
(prostaglandins and leukotrienes), which have a pivotal role in the inflammatory response (4,9).
The inflammatory potential of soybean–based 脂肪 emulsions is demonstrated by one of the
animal models for the acute respiratory distress syndrome (an inflammatory lung injury) which
involves the infusion of oleic acid (an omega-9 fatty acid in 大豆油) into the pulmonary
arteries (20). This would explain why 脂肪 infusions in 全靜脈營養（TPN） formulations are associated with
impaired oxygenation, and can prolong the 呼吸衰竭 (21,22). The omega-3 脂肪酸 in
魚油 have anti-inflammatory activity, and some of the newer 脂肪 formulations mentioned
earlier (e.g., Smof脂肪®) have incorporated 魚油s to take advantage of this activity.

Hepatic Steatosis
Fat accumulation in the liver (肝脂肪浸潤) can occur, but is more common in patients
receiving long-term 全靜脈營養（TPN）. Contributing factors include the ratio of omega-6 to omega-3 fatty
acids in the 脂肪 emulsion, and the content of phytosterols (plant sterols) in 脂肪 emulsions (23).
The 肝脂肪浸潤 can progress to a chronic hepatitis and even cirrhosis if the 全靜脈營養（TPN） is very
prolonged (24).

Cholestasis
全靜脈營養（TPN） is also associated with 膽汁滯留, which can lead to acalculous 膽囊炎 (24). This is
attributed to the absence of 脂肪s in the proximal small bowel, which prevents cholecystokinin-
mediated contraction of the gallbladder.

Lipid Overload Syndrome
The 脂肪 overload syndrome is an uncommon condition that occurs when the 脂肪-induced
increase in serum 三酸甘油酯 overwhelms the ability to break down the 三酸甘油酯, resulting
in a clinical syndrome that can include fever, headache, irritability, respiratory distress, jaundice,
胰臟炎, pancytopenia, and coagulopathy (25,26). This is reported more frequently in
children, and it can resolve when the 脂肪 infusions are temporarily discontinued (26).

The 重新進食症候群 was first described in World War II, when aggressive feeding of
malnourished prisoners of war and concentration camp victims resulted in considerable
morbidity and even mortality.

Predisposing Conditions
The following conditions have been identified as high-risk for the 重新進食症候群 (12):
weight loss of 5% in one month, or >10% in 6 months, body mass index (BMI) ≤18.5 kg/m2,
poor nutrient intake for the past 5 days, low baseline levels of serum potassium, phosphate, or
magnesium, or any patient with a high risk of malnutrition (e.g., from drug abuse).

Recommendations
The following recommendations are for patients who are high-risk for the 重新進食症候群
(12):

1. Before nutrition support is started, electrolyte abnormalities should be corrected, and thiamine
should be started. The American Society of Parenteral and Enteral Nutrition recommends 100–
200 mg thiamine daily (12), but this is inadequate for correcting thiamine deficiency (27), and
the replacement protocol in Table 48.4 is more appropriate.

2. Nutrition support should be started at 40–50% of the estimated nutritional needs (or 10–15
kcal/kg per day), or 葡萄糖 infusions can be started at 150 grams daily (which is 300 mL of
D50). The nutrition regimen is then slowly advanced over the next 4–7 days, while closely
monitoring the serum levels of phosphate, potassium, and magnesium.

### Peripheral 腸胃外營養（Parenteral Nutrition）

Peripheral 腸胃外營養 (PPN) is a truncated form of 全靜脈營養（TPN） that can be used to provide
non蛋白質 熱量 in amounts that will spare the breakdown of 蛋白質s to provide energy (i.e.,
蛋白質-sparing nutrition support). PPN can be used as a supplement to enteral feeding, or as a
source of 熱量 during brief periods of inadequate nutrition. It is not intended for
hypercatabolic or malnourished patients, who need full nutritional support,

The 滲透壓 of 周邊靜脈 infusates should be kept below 900 mosm/L, with a pH
between 7.2 and 7.4, to minimize the risk of vascular damage (28,29). This requires dilute amino
acid and 葡萄糖（dextrose） solutions, which limits nutrient intake. However, 脂肪 emulsions are isotonic

to plasma, and 脂肪s can be used to provide a considerable portion of the non蛋白質 熱量 in
PPN.

Method
A popular regimen for PPN is a mixture of 3% 胺基酸s and 20% 葡萄糖（dextrose） (final
concentration of 1.5% 胺基酸s and 10% 葡萄糖（dextrose）), which has an 滲透壓 of 500 mosm/L.
The 葡萄糖（dextrose） will provide 340 kcal/L, so 2.5 L of the mixture will provide 850 kcal. If 250 mL of
20% Intra脂肪 is added to the regimen (adding 500 kcal), the total non蛋白質 熱量 will
increase to 1,350 kcal/day. This should be close to the non蛋白質 calorie requirement of an
average size, unstressed adult (20 kcal/kg/day).
