## 第25章：氧氣吸入

I brought fire into your midst, and it consumed you, and reduced you
to ashes.

Ezekiel (28:17)

There is a fondness for 氧氣 that is unmatched by any other treatment modality, as is clearly
evident in the unbridled use of 氧氣 in hospitalized patients. The popularity of 氧氣 is
apparent in emergency rooms, where inhaled 氧氣 is a knee-jerk response to acute illness, and
in ICUs, where a patient who is NOT connected to a source of 氧氣 is a rare sight. 氧氣（Oxygen） is
given without evidence of tissue need, and without regard for the damaging effects of 氧氣.
This latter disregard is perplexing, since we protect our food from 氧氣 (with vacuum sealing,
tightly-sealed plastic containers, and cellophane wrapping), yet we don’t have the same
consideration for our patients.

This chapter begins with an examination of the indications and effects of 氧氣 inhalation,
and then describes the different delivery systems for 氧氣. The final section is devoted to the
dark side of 氧氣: i.e., 氧氣-related tissue injury.

### Indications

The first clinical practice guideline for O2 therapy was published in 1984 (1), and the indication
for O2 therapy was stated as follows:

Supplemental 氧氣 therapy is appropriate in acute conditions when there is laboratory documentation of an arterial
PO2 (arterial PO2（PaO2）) <60 mm Hg or an arterial O2 saturation (arterial O2 saturation（SaO2）) <90%; tissue hypoxia is commonly assumed to be present
at these laboratory values [italics mine].

This statement is consistent with the consensus definition of 低血氧症 as a arterial PO2（PaO2） <60 mm Hg
or an arterial O2 saturation（SaO2） <90%. The most recent guidelines for O2 therapy (2) include a target O2 saturation by pulse oximetry（SpO2） (i.e., O2
saturation by pulse oximetry) of 88−92% for patients with CO2 retention, and 90−94% for other
patients. The major issue with this recommendation is the assumption that tissue 氧氣ation is
impaired when the arterial PO2（PaO2） falls below 60 mm Hg, or the O2 saturation by pulse oximetry（SpO2） falls below 90%. The following
sections shed some light on this issue

Hypobaric Hypoxemia
Tolerance to severe 低血氧症 was also demonstrated in a study of healthy individuals who
were placed in a decompression chamber to simulate the atmospheric pressure at the Summit of
Mount Everest (which is 253 mm Hg, or about one-third the pressure at sea level) (5). At this
pressure, the arterial PO2（PaO2） was 30 mm Hg and the arterial O2 saturation（SaO2） was 58% (mean values), but the plasma lactate
level remained in the normal range at 1.7 mmol/L. Similar findings have been reported in
climbers near the summit of Mount Everest without supplemental 氧氣 (6).

Reinhold Messner
One of the most notable challenges to the dogma about severe 低血氧症 came from Reinhold
Messner, the most celebrated mountaineer in the history of the discipline, who was the first to
climb Mount Everest (elevation 29,029 feet) without the use of supplemental 氧氣 (a feat he
achieved in 1978, along with fellow climber Peter Habeler). Prior to the historic climb, Messner
was warned by experts that attempting to summit Mount Everest without supplemental O2 would
be suicidal, as he would lose consciousness and suffer permanent brain damage above an altitude
of 26,000 feet (the region known as the “death zone”). However, Messner knew this was an
untested claim (like many about 氧氣), so he devised a simple test: i.e., using a plane with a
depressurized cabin, he was flown to the summit of Mount Everest while breathing ambient air.
To the disbelief of the experts, Messner experienced no ill effects as the plane climbed above
26,000 feet (the death zone) and reached its destination. He later wrote, “I had seen the flight
through without an 氧氣 mask, and I was still able to talk, to think, to sense everything” (7).

Implications
In summary, there is considerable evidence indicating that O2 inhalation is not based on tissue
O2 needs; a notion that is supported by the physiological effects of O2 described in the next
section. As a result, the use of O2 inhalation based on current recommendations results in
excessive 氧氣ation, and this can be detrimental by promoting oxidative tissue injury, as
described in the latter part of the chapter.

### Physiological Effects

Metabolic Rate
The goal of O2 inhalation is to promote aerobic metabolism. However, there is no evidence that
aerobic metabolism is impaired at the threshold for O2 inhalation, so it seems unlikely that O2
inhalation will have any impact on aerobic metabolism. This is demonstrated in Figure 25.2,

which shows the effects of breathing 24% and 28% 氧氣 on the arterial PO2 (arterial PO2（PaO2）) and the
rate of aerobic metabolism (aerobic metabolism（VO2）) in patients with 急性惡化 of 慢性阻塞性 lung
disease (8). There is a significant increase in the arterial PO2（PaO2） with each increment in inhaled O2, but the
rate of aerobic metabolism remains unchanged. Similar results have been reported in other
clinical studies (9,10), indicating that 氧氣 inhalation does not promote aerobic metabolism.

氧氣（Oxygen） as a Vasoconstrictor
氧氣（Oxygen） acts as a 血管收縮劑 in all major organs except the lungs (11), where it acts as a
血管擴張劑. The principal mechanism for the vasoconstriction is loss of the vasodilating actions
of nitric oxide, which is oxidized by one of the 氧氣 metabolites, the 超氧陰離子（superoxide） radical (see
later) (12). This vasoconstriction has the following consequences:

1. 氧氣（Oxygen） promotes vasoconstriction in the coronary arteries, and can decrease coronary blood

flow in patients with coronary artery disease (13).

2. 氧氣（Oxygen）-induced systemic vasoconstriction can decrease the 心輸出量 and promote tissue
hypoperfusion, and this effect counteracts the ability of O2 inhalation to increase systemic O2
delivery (14).

3. In animal studies, 氧氣 can completely obliterate capillary networks in skeletal muscle (15).
Implications
The observation that O2 inhalation does not promote aerobic metabolism is consistent with the
notion that the clinical use of O2 is not based on tissue O2 needs. Furthermore, 氧氣-induced
vasoconstriction can be viewed as an inherent mechanism that protects the tissues from excess
(unwanted) 氧氣. In other words, the tissues have enough O2, and they don’t want anymore.

### 氧氣（Oxygen） Delivery Systems

A variety of 氧氣 delivery systems are available for spontaneously breathing patients, and the
major ones are listed in Table 25.2, along with some characteristic features of each.

Flow Rate and fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2）
Supplemental O2 is provided as a pure gas that is delivered into the mouth or nasopharynx at a
preselected flow rate, and the fractional concentration of inhaled O2 (fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2）) is determined by the
balance between the rate of O2 delivery and the patient’s inspiratory flow rate. (There are some
exceptions to this, as described later.) If the patient’s inspiratory flow rate exceeds the rate of O2
delivery, the excess flow will draw in room air, which will decrease the fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2）. A normal
inspiratory flow rate is about 15 L/min (0.25 L/sec) during quiet breathing, but it can be as high
as 120 L/min in patients with 急性呼吸衰竭 (16). Therefore, low-flow O2 delivery
systems (which have delivery rates below 15 L/min) are usually inadequate for patients with
急性呼吸衰竭, especially those with 呼吸窘迫 or labored breathing.

Low-Flow Systems
Low-flow systems deliver O2 at rates of ≤15 L/min, and are most suitable for patients with mild
低血氧症 and no 呼吸窘迫.

Low-Flow Nasal O2
Low flow O2 is typically delivered via 鼻導管 at flow rates of 1–6 L/min (although rates up
to 15 L/min are possible with large-bore nasal cannulas). The O2 is usually humidified when the
flow rate exceeds 4 L/min. The fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） during quiet breathing is about 24% (at 1 L/min) up to 40%
(at 6 L/min).

The major advantages of low-flow nasal O2 are simplicity of use and patient acceptance,
including the ability for patients to eat and converse. The major disadvantage is the inability to
achieve high concentrations of inhaled O2.

Standard Face Masks
Standard face masks deliver 氧氣 at flow rates between 5 and 10 L/min (a minimum flow rate
of 5 L/min is needed to clear exhaled gas from the mask). Exhalation ports on the side of the face
mask also allow room air to be inhaled. This system can achieve a maximum fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） of about 60%
during quiet breathing. Although face masks can deliver a slightly higher fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） than 鼻導管,
they are more confining, and they do not permit oral feeding.

High-flow systems include reservoir masks, air-entrainment devices, specialized “diffuser”

Reservoir Masks
Reservoir masks are standard face masks that are attached to a 儲氣囊 that is filled with
氧氣 from a constant-flow 氧氣 source. If the 儲氣囊 is kept inflated, the patient will
draw primarily from the gas in the bag, which allows for higher concentrations of inhaled
氧氣.

An example of a reservoir mask is shown in Figure 25.3. This is a 非再呼吸式面罩,
which is designed to prevent rebreathing of exhaled CO2. The 儲氣囊 is filled with 氧氣,

Non-rebreather masks were once a popular method for providing high concentrations of O2,

Air Entrainment Masks
Air entrainment masks are designed to deliver a constant fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2）. The operation of an air-

and this keeps the fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） constant. The fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） is varied by varying the size of the air entrainment
ports. Although 空氣混入式面罩s use O2 flow rates of ≤15 L/min, they are considered high-
flow systems because the total flow (i.e., O2 flow plus room air flow) is often ≥60 L/min. The
fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） range of these devices is 24 to 50%.

Air-entrainment masks have been popular for patients with chronic CO2 retention, where an

inadvertent increase in fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） can lead to further increases in arterial PCO2. However, their
popularity has waned, because the continuous monitoring of arterial O2 saturation with pulse
oximetry is a deterrent to excessive 氧氣ation in patients with CO2 retention.

The OxyMask™™
The Oxymask™ (Southmedic, Inc., Barrie, Ontario, Canada) is equipped with a proprietary

The OxyMask™ has outperformed the 非再呼吸式面罩 (less CO2 rebreathing) (18) and the
空氣混入式面罩 (greater O2 concentration at lower flows) (19). It is a superior mask for
delivering high concentrations of O2, at a relatively low cost (because of the low flow rate for
O2). However this mask otherwise offers no advantages over the 高流量鼻導管氧氣（HFNO） system
described next.

High-flow nasal O2 (HFNO) is the greatest advance in O2 delivery systems in recent memory.
The basic design of the HFNO circuit is illustrated in Figure 25.5. This circuit provides heated

### 氧氣（Oxygen） As A Source Of Injury

The introduction to this chapter included a statement about protecting our food from the 氧氣
in atmospheric air (e.g., with vacuum sealing), and the reason is that 氧氣 decomposes organic
matter by disrupting organic molecules (including carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids). The
chemical derivatives of 氧氣, which are called reactive 氧氣 species (ROS) are even more
damaging than the parent molecule, and are capable of inflicting lethal cell injury (27). In fact,
contrary to the popular perception that 氧氣 protects cells from injury in critically ill patients,
the accumulating evidence indicates that 氧氣 (via the production of ROS) is an important
source of cell injury in critically ill patients (27–29). (The role of ROS in inflammation is
described in Chapter 17.) The following is a brief description of the injurious nature of 氧氣.

氧氣（Oxygen） Metabolism
The metabolism of O2 takes place at the end of the electron transport chain in mitochondria
(within the cytochrome oxidase complex), where the electrons that accumulate in the process of
ATP production are cleared by adding them to O2 to produce water. (The addition of electrons to
an atom or molecule is known as chemical reduction.) The O2 molecule can accept four
electrons, but only one electron can be added per reduction reaction (the reason for this is beyond
the scope of this chapter), which results in the reaction sequence shown in Figure 25.6.

The intermediates in the reduction of O2 to water include the 超氧陰離子（superoxide） radical, hydrogen
peroxide, and the 氫氧自由基. All are reactive 氧氣 species (ROS) that are capable of
disrupting and damaging vital cell components (e.g., membrane lipids, cytoplasmic proteins, and
DNA) by the chemical process of oxidation (which removes electrons from a substrate). Thus,
ROS are sometimes referred to as oxidizing agents, or oxidants. Some relevant features of the
ROS generated by O2 metabolism are summarized below (27).

1. The 超氧陰離子（superoxide） and 氫氧自由基s are free radicals (i.e., they have an unpaired electron in

their outer orbitals), and are highly reactive.

2. Hydrogen peroxide is not a free radical, but it is a powerful oxidizing agent that readily moves

across cell membranes, and can be widely distributed throughout the body.

3. The 氫氧自由基 is the most reactive molecule known in biochemistry, and enters into a
reaction within three molecular diameters of its point of origin. It is the most destructive of all
the ROS, and is a major source of oxidant-induced cell injury.
4. Free iron in its reduced form (Fe++) catalyzes the formation of the 氫氧自由基, and thus

free iron can act as a pro-oxidant (see later).

Normally, at least 95% of 氧氣 is completely reduced to water, and only a very small fraction
(about 3%) of O2 metabolism generates damaging ROS (27). However, there is a more
important, source of ROS: i.e., the inflammatory response (see next).

Neutrophil Activation
As described in Chapter 17 (see Figure 17.1), the activation of 嗜中性球s involves a precipitous
rise (up to 20-fold) in O2 consumption (30), which is known as the 呼吸爆發, and is
designed to generate ROS. Neutrophils also have a 髓過氧化酶 enzyme that generates
hypochlorite, which is the active ingredient in household bleach.

Inflammatory Injury
There is abundant evidence that ROS have a prominent role in the tissue injury attributed to
inflammation (see Chapter 17) (31). The damaging effects of ROS are accelerated by the
tendency for free radicals to create chain reactions (32). When a free radical reacts with a non-
radical, the non-radical loses an electron and is transformed into a free radical, which can then
remove an electron from another nonradical to produce another free radical, and so on. This
creates a self-sustaining reaction or chain reaction, and these reactions are troublesome because
they continue after the inciting event is eliminated. (A fire is a familiar example of a chain
reaction involving free radicals.) Chain reactions would explain the progression of inflammatory
multiorgan damage in severe sepsis and septic shock after the infection has been eradicated.

Superoxide dismutase (超氧化物歧化酶（SOD）) is an enzyme that facilitates the conversion of 超氧陰離子（superoxide） radicals
to 過氧化氫. 超氧化物歧化酶（SOD） is considered an 抗氧化劑 because it clears the 超氧陰離子（superoxide） radical,
but it also increases the production of 過氧化氫, and thus it can also act as a pro-

The role of 超氧化物歧化酶（SOD） in inflammatory injury is suggested by animal studies of pain perception,

notion that is supported by evidence that 超氧化物歧化酶（SOD） is inactivated by products of inflammation (35).
These findings have important implications for the development of nonopioid analgesics for
painful inflammation.

Glutathione is present in high concentrations is most mammalian cells, and is synthesized de

fold higher than plasma (39), suggesting that 穀胱甘肽（glutathione） plays an important role in protecting the
lung from oxidant injury. There is evidence that 穀胱甘肽（glutathione） is depleted in critically ill patients
(40).

toxicity, which is attributed to 穀胱甘肽（glutathione） deficiency - see Chapter 52). N-乙醯半胱胺酸（N-acetylcysteine） has a
promising future as an 抗氧化劑 in critically ill patients (42).

SELENIUM: Selenium is an essential trace element that serves as a cofactor for the 穀胱甘肽（glutathione）
peroxidase enzyme in humans. The recommended dietary allowance (RDA) for 硒（selenium） is 55
µg daily in adult men and women (43). The absence of dietary 硒（selenium） produces measurable
decreases in 穀胱甘肽（glutathione） peroxidase activity after just one week (44). Selenium levels in blood
are typically low in critically ill patients (45), and high-dose 硒（selenium） replacement (1000 µg IV
daily) has been associated with improved survival in patients with severe sepsis and septic shock
(45).

Selenium status can be monitored using whole blood 硒（selenium） (normal range = 0.96–1.78
µmol/L) or serum 硒（selenium） (normal range = 0.72–1.33 µmol/L). If needed, 硒（selenium） can be
given intravenously as sodium selenite. The highest dose that is considered safe is 200 µg daily.

Vitamin E
Vitamin E (alpha-tocopherol) is a lipid-soluble vitamin that is found in the interior of most cell
membranes, where it serves as a “chain breaking” 抗氧化劑 to halt the progression of lipid
peroxidation (i.e., the oxidation of polyunsaturated fatty acids, also known as rancidity when it
occurs in food products), which proceeds as a chain reaction. To accomplish this, 維生素E
donates an electron to a free radical intermediate in 脂質過氧化, and in so doing, 維生素E
becomes a free radical, but an innocuous one. This reaction halts the chain reaction of lipid
peroxidation. (Note: Lipid peroxidation is initiated by the 氫氧自由基, and Figure 25.7
shows Vitamin E blocking this reaction, to indicate that Vitamin E blocks 脂質過氧化.)

Vitamin E depletion has been reported in patients with acute 呼吸窘迫 syndrome
(46), and there is evidence that high-dose 維生素E has beneficial effects in trauma victims (see
later). The normal concentration of 維生素E in plasma is 1 mg/dL, and a level below 0.5 mg/dL
is evidence of deficiency (47).

Vitamin C
Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is a water-soluble vitamin that is best known for its essential role in
collagen formation. However, it is also a reducing agent that can donate electrons to free radicals
to erase their adverse effects. As a result, ascorbate can act as a “scavenger” for 氧氣-derived
free radicals: i.e., 超氧陰離子（superoxide） radicals and 氫氧自由基s (48). Ascorbate also donates
electrons to regenerate Vitamin E from the inactive Vitamin E radical.

Ceruloplasmin and Transferrin
Ceruloplasmin and 轉鐵蛋白（transferrin） account for most of the 抗氧化劑 activity in plasma (49). The
抗氧化劑 activity of both proteins is related to their actions in limiting free iron in the reduced
form (Fe2+), which will limit the production of 氫氧自由基s. Ceruloplasmin oxidizes iron
from the Fe2+ to the Fe3+ state, and 轉鐵蛋白（transferrin） binds iron in the oxidized or Fe3+ state. The role
of free iron as a pro-oxidant (50) may explain why most of the iron in the body is bound to
proteins or sequestered (e.g., in the bone marrow).

Oxidative Stress
The risk of oxidative tissue injury is determined by the balance between oxidant and 抗氧化劑
activities. When oxidant activity exceeds the neutralizing capacity of the 抗氧化劑s, the excess
or unopposed oxidant activity can promote tissue injury. This condition of unopposed biological
oxidation is known as 氧化壓力, and it is the principal source of oxidative tissue injury.
The most familiar example of 氧化壓力 is severe or persistent inflammation, especially in
critically ill patients, which combines an increase in production of oxidants (ROS) with likely
depletion of 抗氧化劑s.

Monitoring 氧化壓力 has obvious benefits for the care of critically ill patients (or any
patient with inflammatory tissue injury). One promising method involves the measurement of
脂質過氧化 products in exhaled gas (51), but this methodology is not currently available in
clinical practice.

### Pulmonary 氧氣（Oxygen） Toxicity

Pulmonary 氧氣 toxicity is an inflammatory lung injury (similar to the acute respiratory
distress syndrome described in Chapter 24) that occurs in response to the prolonged inhalation of
high concentrations of 氧氣. The following are some relevant issues regarding this form of
oxidative injury.

Species Differences
The tendency to develop pulmonary 氧氣 toxicity varies in different species. For example,
laboratory rats will die of respiratory failure after 5 to 7 days of breathing 100% O2, while cold-
blooded species like sea turtles can breathe pure O2 for several weeks and even months without
harm (52). Non-human primates, which are closest to humans, show signs of pulmonary O2
toxicity after about one week of breathing pure 氧氣 (53).

Human Studies
In healthy volunteers, inhalation of 100% O2 for 6 to 12 hours results in a 氣管支氣管炎 and
a decrease in 肺活量 attributed to 吸收性肺膨脹不全 (54). Prolonged exposure to 100%
O2 has been reported in only 6 humans: 5 with irreversible coma who received 100% O2 for 3 to
4 days (55), and one healthy volunteer who inhaled pure O2 for 4.5 days (56). In all these cases,
the subjects developed a pulmonary condition that was consistent with inflammatory lung injury.

What fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） is Toxic?
Based on the observation of a decreased 肺活量 when the fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） exceeds 60% (54), the
threshold fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） for pulmonary O2 toxicity was set at 60%. However, adopting a single threshold
fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） for all patients neglects the contribution of endogenous 抗氧化劑s to the risk of 氧氣
toxicity. For example, animal studies have shown that pulmonary O2 toxicity is exacerbated by
維生素E deficiency (57) and 穀胱甘肽（glutathione） deficiency (58). Since 抗氧化劑 depletion seems to
be common in ICU patients (40,45,46), it is reasonable to assume that the toxic level of fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） is
much lower than suspected in critically ill patients. Therefore, the best practice is keeping the
fractional concentration of inhaled O2（FIO2） at the lowest tolerable level.

Promoting Antioxidant Protection
Another approach to minimizing the risk of pulmonary O2 toxicity (and any source of oxidative
cell injury) is to monitor and correct 抗氧化劑 levels. This approach is supported by evidence
of 抗氧化劑 depletion in critically ill patients (40,45,46) Evidence of benefit from such an
approach is provided by a study in trauma patients (59), which showed that a high-dose
抗氧化劑 cocktail of 維生素C (1000 mg every 8 hrs), 維生素E (1000 units every 8 hrs) and
硒（selenium） (200 µg daily) for 7 days was associated with a significant decline in cases of
respiratory failure and ventilator dependence. Promoting 抗氧化劑 protection for your patients
is similar to the use of tightly sealed plastic containers to protect your food.

### A Final Word

Why is 氧氣（Oxygen） a Vasoconstrictor?
For those who like to consider 目的論 in biological design, the actions of 氧氣 to promote
systemic vasoconstriction merits some attention.

The ability of inhaled O2 to augment tissue 氧氣ation is determined by its influence on
both the O2 content in arterial blood (arterial O2 content（CaO2）), and the 心輸出量 (CO), as defined by the
equation for arterial O2 delivery: i.e.,

O2 Delivery = arterial O2 content（CaO2） × CO

(25.3)

Inhaled O2 will increase the arterial O2 content（CaO2）, but the O2-induced systemic vasoconstriction leads to a
decrease in CO, and the net effect is that O2 delivery remains unchanged (60). This would
explain why O2 inhalation has no effect on aerobic metabolism, as shown in Figure 25.2.
The 血管收縮劑 response to O2 can then be viewed as a defense mechanism that is
designed to protect the tissues from excess 氧氣 (i.e., more than is needed to maintain aerobic
metabolism). This will limit the risk of oxidative tissue injury, and in this sense, the
血管收縮劑 response to O2 can be viewed as an 抗氧化劑 defense mechanism.

The human body seems designed to protect the vital organs from 氧氣, and our liberal use

of 氧氣 is the contrary to human design.
